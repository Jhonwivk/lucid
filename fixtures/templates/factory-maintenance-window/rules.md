# Meridian Plant — maintenance window rules

Fictional fixture.

1. At most one high-criticality production line may be offline at the same time.
2. The mechanical team can support two concurrent work orders.
3. Shared crane C-1 serves Line-A and Line-B; overlapping crane-required jobs are prohibited.
4. Night-shift work requires an approved crew roster.
5. Production freeze on Thursday 10:00–14:00 overrides preferred maintenance windows.
