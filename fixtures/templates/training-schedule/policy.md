# Northstar Learning — scheduling policy

Fictional training-planning fixture.

- Planning horizon is Monday through Friday.
- An instructor may teach at most two sessions in one day.
- A room must have capacity greater than or equal to registered demand.
- Rooms Atlas and Birch cannot host two sessions at the same time.
- Sessions ending after 18:00 require explicit overtime approval.
- Keep the same cohort in one room where practical; room changes are undesirable but not forbidden.
